FAQ

Common questions

How long does a detail take?

Most exterior or interior packages take 1–3 hours. A full detail typically takes 3–5 hours, and paint correction or ceramic coating can take a full day or more.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Let us know your setup when booking and we'll confirm what's needed for your location.

Why does price vary by vehicle?

Larger vehicles and those with heavy dirt, pet hair, or stains take more time and product. We'll always confirm the price before starting.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Depending on the coating chosen and how it's maintained, 1 to 5+ years.

How does the referral reward work?

When someone books and puts your name in the "Referred by" box, they get $15 off and you get a $15 credit toward your next detail. Credits stack — refer three friends, save $45.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash, card and common payment apps. Payment is due when the job is complete.
