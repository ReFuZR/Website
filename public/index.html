Questions people ask

How long does it take?

An exterior or interior is usually 1 to 3 hours. A full detail is around 3 to 5. Paint correction and ceramic coating can take most of a day.

What area do you cover?

Harrisonburg and the rest of Rockingham County. If you're a little outside that, text me and ask.

Do I need to have water or power?

Let me know what you've got when you book and I'll tell you what I need.

Why does the price change for bigger vehicles?

More surface, more seats, more time. Really dirty cars take longer too. You'll always know the price before I start.

How long does ceramic coating last?

The one I offer is rated for about a year. Longer-lasting coatings are available if you want them. How you wash the car makes a big difference too, and I'll show you how.

How does the referral deal work?

Your friend puts your name in the "Referred by" box when they book. They get $15 off, and you get $15 off your next detail. Refer three people, save $45.

Will my tint be legal?

Yes. I only install shades that meet Virginia law, and I'll help you pick one that looks good and won't get you pulled over.

How do I pay?

Cash, card or the usual payment apps. You pay when the job's done.
